# Idea: One Park website — line-drawing ride-through video

**Status:** Parked. No work needed yet.
**Filed:** 2026-10-14

## The concept

- **Reference:** a shader video of a motorbike riding through a city — https://www.shadertoy.com/view/fXc3RB
- **Look:** make it a line-drawing version — black and white, no colour.
- **Buildings:** the city the bike rides through is built from the wire mesh / wire box artwork (the same wire pieces used on the Bro You Want Art site).
- **Sound:** keep the song.
- **Lyrics:** cheesy karaoke-style lyrics along the bottom of the screen.

## Add-on: the site's opening image (added 2026-10-14)

- **Reference:** `refs/one-park-opening-image-ref.webp`. It's a coloured-pencil drawing: a figure made of dense looping lines, with hundreds of coloured strands streaming off to the right and turning into handwritten names in Korean and English.
- **Idea:** the site's opening image could be an evolution of this drawing.
- **Possible link to the video:** the looping strands could unravel into the line-drawing city, or the video could end by winding back into the figure.
